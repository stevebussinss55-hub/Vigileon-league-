Delete your Vigileon League account

You can ask us to delete your Vigileon League account and personal information at any time. Here is how.

Option 1: From the app

  1. Open the Vigileon League app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email (no app needed)

Send an email to tweneboahgideon55@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. Send it from the email address linked to your Vigileon League account, and include your gamertag so we can find your account.

We will confirm once your account has been deleted.

What gets deleted

Your account, gamertag, profile details, messages you sent in the app, and personal information we hold about you.

What we may keep

Some records may be kept where the law requires it, or where needed to settle a payout, dispute or fraud case. Payment records are kept by Paystack and our payment records as required by law.

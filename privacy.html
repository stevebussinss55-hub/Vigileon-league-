Privacy Policy

Vigileon League · Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Vigileon League (“we”, “us”) collects when you use the Vigileon League app and website at league.vigileongh.com, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your gamertag, and your password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, not in readable form).
  • Gameplay information: tournament entries, match results, win and loss records, and match screenshots or videos you submit as proof.
  • Messages: messages you send in the app, including challenge chat and direct messages.
  • Profile information: any avatar or profile details you choose to add.
  • Payment and payout details: when you pay an entry fee or request a payout, payment is handled by Paystack. We receive a payment reference and the amount. If you request a payout, we also receive the Mobile Money number you provide. We do not store your card details.
  • Identity verification: where we require it, a photo of an ID document and a selfie, used only to confirm who you are.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a device token so we can send you push notifications.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To run tournaments, record results, and check match proof.
  • To process entry fees and payouts, and to prevent fraud and fake results.
  • To send you service messages, such as results, reviews and payout updates, and to send notifications you have chosen.
  • To respond to your feedback, reports and disputes.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (database, storage, sign-in and notifications)
  • Paystack (payments and payouts)
  • An email service provider (to send service emails)
  • Other players, where needed for matches, such as your gamertag and match results shown on tournament and leaderboard pages.

4. Children

Vigileon League is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed to run tournaments, handle payouts and disputes, and meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices and rights

You can update your profile in the app, turn off notifications in your device settings, and ask us for a copy of your information.

7. Deleting your account

You can request deletion of your account and personal information at any time:

  • In the app: go to Profile and choose Delete account.
  • By web: see league.vigileongh.com/delete-account.html, or email tweneboahgideon55@gmail.com

Some information may be kept where the law requires it, or where it is needed to resolve a payout, dispute or fraud case.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure cloud services. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the “Last updated” date above when we do.
